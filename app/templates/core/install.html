Put {{ site_name }} on your phone

Add {{ site_name }} to your Home Screen and it opens like an app: full screen, with its own icon. You don't need an app store, and it updates itself.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari. Chrome, Edge and Firefox work too on iOS 16.4 or later.
  2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up. In recent versions of Safari it's in the ⋯ menu at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on if you see it, then tap Add.
  5. Open {{ site_name }} from the new icon and sign in. Your phone keeps this sign-in separate from Safari, so you sign in once more here.

Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.

If {{ site_name }} offers an Install button on your home page, that does the same thing.
