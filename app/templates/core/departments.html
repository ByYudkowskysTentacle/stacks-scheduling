Departments

Each department plans its own schedules with its own roster and, if you set them, its own coverage: people on duty here, and coverage overrides and skill rules under Scheduling rules. Someone can be in several departments; their hours across all of them count toward their caps. Put people in departments from their record in People, and give supervisors their departments under Roles. Admins see every department.
